Important disclosure

Background location is used for anti-theft protection

When Anti-Theft Protection is enabled and the required Android permissions are granted, the protected phone may collect and upload precise location information even while the app is not actively open. This allows the authenticated owner to locate a lost or stolen device, view its last known position, and use Live Find.

01

About this policy

Batras Industries ("we", "us", or "our") provides Batras Anti-Theft Protection, an Android application designed to help users protect and locate devices that they have registered to their own account.

This policy explains what information the app processes, why it is processed, how it supports anti-theft features, and what choices are available to users.

02

Information we process

Account information

Name, email address, authentication provider, Firebase user identifier, and information required to authenticate your account.

Protected-device information

App-generated device ID, device name, protected-device UID, owner UID, and protection status.

Precise location

Latitude, longitude, location accuracy, speed when available, and last-seen information.

Device status

Battery level, online/heartbeat state, tracking mode, and whether Stolen Mode is active.

Remote commands

Command IDs and timestamps used for Remote Ring, Remote Lock, Live Find, and Stolen Mode.

Local security settings

Protection/PIN configuration may be stored locally on the device. The app is designed to store a PIN as a cryptographic hash rather than plain text.

03

Background and precise location

Background location is a core anti-theft function. When you enable Anti-Theft Protection and grant the required location permissions, the protected phone may continue sending location and heartbeat updates while the app is in the background.

This information is used so the authenticated owner can locate a device after it is lost or stolen, view a current or last-known location, and use Live Find.

You can revoke location permissions from Android system settings. If precise or background location permission is removed, location-based anti-theft features may stop working or become less accurate.

04

How we use information

  • Authenticate users through supported sign-in methods.
  • Register and link a protected Android device to its owner account.
  • Show current or last-known device location.
  • Show battery, online/offline, last-seen, accuracy, and tracking status.
  • Support Live Find while protection is enabled.
  • Deliver owner-authorized Remote Ring and Remote Lock commands.
  • Enable or disable Stolen Mode for a protected device.
  • Maintain security, reliability, and integrity of the anti-theft service.
05

Third-party services

The app uses third-party services to provide authentication, cloud storage, location, and map functionality. Depending on the feature you use, these may include:

  • Google Firebase Authentication — account authentication.
  • Google Cloud Firestore / Firebase — protected-device records, location/status information, and remote-command state.
  • Google Play services location APIs — device location when permission is granted.
  • Google Identity / Google Sign-In — when you choose Google authentication.
  • MapLibre / OpenFreeMap — map display used by the Live Find interface.

These service providers may process technical information under their own privacy policies and terms.

06

Sharing and sale of personal information

We do not sell personal information. Information may be processed by the service providers listed above only as needed to operate app functionality, or disclosed where required by applicable law.

07

Security

We use authentication, protected-device ownership checks, database access controls, and other reasonable safeguards intended to limit access to protected-device information to authorized users.

No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure, so absolute security cannot be guaranteed.

08

Data retention

Account and protected-device data may be retained while the account or linked device remains active and for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service, address security or abuse, resolve disputes, or meet legal obligations.

Users can request deletion of their account and associated cloud data using the process described below.

09

Your choices and controls

  • Grant or revoke Android permissions from system settings.
  • Disable Anti-Theft Protection using the app's available security controls.
  • Choose from the supported sign-in methods.
  • Request deletion of your account and associated cloud data.
10

Delete account & data

You may request deletion of your Batras Anti-Theft Protection account and associated cloud data.

How to request deletion

  1. Contact Batras Industries using the developer contact email shown on the app's Google Play Store listing.
  2. Use the subject: Delete Batras Anti-Theft Account.
  3. Send the request from the email address registered with your Batras Anti-Theft Protection account.
  4. Include your registered email address and, if available, your Tracker ID or protected Device ID.
  5. Never send your password, PIN, Google password, OTP, or other login secret.

After reasonable ownership verification, the account and associated cloud data will be scheduled for deletion. This may include the user profile, linked protected-device records, stored location/last-seen information, battery and tracking status, and remote-command state.

Limited information may be retained where reasonably necessary for security, fraud/abuse prevention, dispute resolution, or legal obligations.

We aim to process verified deletion requests within 30 days.

Important: deleting your account can permanently stop access to Find My Device, Live Find, Remote Ring, Remote Lock, Stolen Mode, and other linked-device features.
